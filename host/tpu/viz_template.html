    About this trace

    Each step is one clock cycle of the RTL, sampled just before the rising edge: every register as it stands, and every input the edge is about to take. A PE's Σ is the multiply-accumulate it performs at that edge. Keys: ← → step, space plays. Link to a cycle with #c<cycle> on the page's address.
